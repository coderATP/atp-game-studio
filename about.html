About ATP

Building from West Africa for the digital world.

ATP Game Studio is a Nigerian software and game development company focused on creating software, digital products and interactive experiences.

Who we are

ATP Game Studio brings software development and game development under one roof. Our work spans web-based applications, digital tools, HTML5 games and other interactive products.

We are interested in the space where technology meets creativity: products that are technically sound, visually distinctive and genuinely useful to the people they are made for.

What we believe

Good digital products should be clear, purposeful and enjoyable to use. We favour thoughtful engineering, continuous improvement and solutions that can grow with their users.

Based in Nigeria

ATP Game Studio operates from Nigeria and builds with a global, internet-first audience in mind.
