THE GAME

One card starts it. The chain takes over.

ChainRxn is a turn-based multiplayer card strategy game built around one simple idea: play a card that connects to the Foundation and keep the chain moving.

HOW IT WORKS

Every move matters.

The rules are simple. The decisions are not. Manage your hand, watch the Foundation and choose when to push your advantage.
