THE GAME

Find the route. Beat the clock.

Dungeon Game is a route-finding challenge built around movement, timing, and efficiency.

Each level places you inside a grid-based dungeon with a starting position and a destination. Walkable spaces allow you to move through the dungeon while walls block your path.

The game calculates the shortest valid route between the start and destination. Your goal is to reach the destination before time runs out while keeping your actual movement as close to that route as possible.

FAQ

Before you enter.

What is the objective?

Start from the marked starting position and reach the destination before your available time runs out.

How does the timer work?

The starting duration is calculated from a base amount of time plus additional time based on the shortest route distance.

What do collectibles do?

Collectibles add their configured time bonus to your remaining time when collected.

What is route efficiency?

Route efficiency compares the optimal route distance with the actual number of movement steps taken during your run.

How is the star rating determined?

The game awards one to five stars based on route efficiency. A perfect shortest route earns five stars.

What happens when time runs out?

Player movement stops and the run reports that time has expired.
