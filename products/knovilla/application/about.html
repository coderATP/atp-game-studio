About Knovilla

Learn. Practice. Challenge yourself.

What is Knovilla?

Knovilla is an educational quiz and assessment platform designed to help students learn, practise, and test their knowledge across a wide range of subjects.

It combines structured educational content with interactive quizzes and game-based experiences to make learning more engaging and enjoyable.

Knovilla is developed by ATP Game Studio.

Who is Knovilla for?

Knovilla is built primarily for students, while also providing tools for examiners who assess students through theory and practical questions.

For Students

Students can practise questions, test their knowledge, track their progress, and challenge themselves through different quiz and game modes.

For Examiners

Examiners can assess student responses to theory and practical questions and grade submitted work.

Learning Levels

Knovilla organises learning content according to educational levels, making it easier for students to practise material appropriate to their stage of education.

  • Primary
  • Junior Secondary
  • Senior Secondary
  • University

Subjects

Knovilla currently focuses on core science and related academic subjects, including:

  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Physics
  • Mathematics

More subjects and learning content may be introduced as Knovilla develops.

Question Styles

Knovilla includes different approaches to assessment, including objective questions, true-or-false questions, theory questions, and practical assessments.

It also provides exam-style sections, including Common Entrance and WAEC-style practice. These sections are designed as practice experiences and are not presented as official examination content or as being affiliated with the examination bodies concerned.

How Knovilla Works

Students can select an appropriate subject, educational level, topic, or quiz mode and begin practising.

Depending on the activity, students may receive immediate results or submit theory and practical responses for examiner assessment.

Knovilla also keeps track of learning progress so students can see how they are performing over time.

Game Mode

Learning on Knovilla isn't limited to traditional quizzes. The platform also includes a game mode designed to make knowledge practice more interactive and competitive.

Multiplayer experiences include pass-and-play, local Wi-Fi, and online multiplayer modes.

What's Ahead?

Knovilla is currently centred around quizzes, assessment, and educational games, but the platform is being developed with a broader learning experience in mind.

More subjects, question styles, learning tools, game features, and educational resources will be introduced as Knovilla continues to grow.
