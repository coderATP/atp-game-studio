Knovilla

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Knovilla is an educational quiz, assessment, and game platform developed by ATP Game Studio ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Knovilla, how that information is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

By using Knovilla, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Knovilla and the features you choose to use.

2.1 Account Information

When you create a registered account, we may collect your username, email address, password, role information, and other information necessary to operate your account.

Passwords are processed using appropriate authentication and security measures. Your password is not displayed to other users.

2.2 Profile Information

Knovilla allows registered users to optionally add a profile picture to customise their experience.

Providing a profile picture is voluntary. Users may choose not to provide one.

2.3 Guest Accounts

Knovilla allows certain features to be used without creating a registered account.

A guest session may use a generated identifier to maintain the session and associate activity with that temporary user.

2.4 Learning and Assessment Data

When you use Knovilla, information related to your learning and assessment activity may be stored. This may include quiz questions, selected answers, scores, results, progress information, submissions, and related assessment data.

Theory and practical responses may be stored so that authorised examiners can review and grade student submissions.

3. Multiplayer Information

Knovilla provides multiplayer experiences including pass-and- play, local Wi-Fi, and online multiplayer modes.

When using multiplayer features, information required to operate the game session may be processed. This can include player identifiers, usernames, room information, game configuration, answers, scores, and other temporary gameplay information required to run the multiplayer session.

Multiplayer gameplay information may be transmitted between your device and the Knovilla multiplayer server while a game session is active.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Knovilla may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage and sessionStorage to maintain login sessions, guest sessions, user identifiers, preferences, and other application-related information.

Information stored in your browser may remain on your device until you log out, remove it, clear your browser data, or the application replaces or removes the stored information.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through Knovilla may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain sessions.
  • Provide quizzes and educational activities.
  • Save and display learning progress and results.
  • Process theory and practical submissions.
  • Allow authorised examiners to assess student submissions.
  • Provide and operate multiplayer features.
  • Display optional user profile information.
  • Maintain and improve Knovilla.
  • Detect and prevent misuse of the platform.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services.

6. How We Store Information

Registered account information, learning records, assessment data, and other server-side information required by Knovilla are stored and processed through our backend infrastructure.

Knovilla currently uses MongoDB with Mongoose for server-side data storage and database management.

Information stored on our servers is retained according to the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and the applicable Knovilla features.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be made available to other users when this is necessary for a feature you intentionally use. For example, multiplayer games may display player usernames and relevant gameplay information to participants in the same game session.

Student theory and practical submissions may be made available to authorised examiners for the purpose of assessment and grading.

We may use technical service providers required to operate Knovilla, including hosting, database, server, communication, and infrastructure services. Such providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or integrity of Knovilla and its users.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Knovilla retains learning and assessment records so that users can access their results and progress and so that submitted theory and practical work can be reviewed and graded.

Users can deliberately delete eligible submissions through the available deletion controls in Knovilla.

When a user deletes a submission, it is first moved to an archived state rather than being immediately and permanently removed.

Archived submissions are permanently deleted after 21 days.

Users can also delete their Knovilla account using the account deletion mechanism provided within the application.

9. Advertising

Knovilla may introduce advertising as part of its monetisation strategy.

If advertising services are introduced, advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable privacy requirements.

This Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary to reflect the advertising technologies and services used by Knovilla.

We will not intentionally place advertising in a manner designed to encourage accidental clicks or interfere with active gameplay.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information handled by Knovilla against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the information involved and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other appropriate action.

You may manage information stored locally by Knovilla through the application's available controls or through your browser's site-data and storage settings.

You may also use Knovilla's account deletion mechanism to delete your account.

For privacy-related questions or requests that cannot be handled through the application, contact us using the email address provided below.

12. Students and Younger Users

Knovilla is designed for educational use and may be used by students of different educational levels, including younger students.

We encourage parents, guardians, schools, and educational institutions to provide appropriate guidance when younger students use online services.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately or that we have collected information that should not have been collected, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

13. International Use

Knovilla is an online service and may be accessed by users from different countries.

Depending on where you are located and where our service providers operate, information may be processed in countries other than your own.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Knovilla develops, new features are introduced, or applicable privacy requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
