Getting Started with Knovilla: A Beginner's Guide

New to Knovilla? This beginner-friendly guide explains how to access the platform, choose an entry option, explore its learning activities and begin making the most of your educational experience.

Knovilla is an educational application developed by ATP Game Studio. It brings together subject-based quizzes, assessments and interactive learning experiences in a browser-accessible environment. Whether you are a student looking to practise academic questions, an educator exploring assessment activities or someone interested in testing their knowledge, understanding how to navigate Knovilla is the first step towards using it effectively.

You do not need to install a separate desktop application to get started. Knovilla can be opened directly through a supported web browser. This guide walks through the essential steps, from entering the platform to selecting your first activity.

1. Open Knovilla in your browser

Begin by visiting the official Knovilla application at knovilla.atpgamestudio.com.ng . Allow the application a moment to load, especially if you are opening it for the first time or using a slower internet connection.

For a comfortable experience, use an up-to-date browser and ensure that JavaScript is enabled. Since Knovilla is a web-based application, your browser is responsible for displaying its interface and running its interactive components.

Once the opening screen appears, you can choose how you would like to enter the platform.

2. Choose an entry option

Knovilla provides different entry options so that users can choose an approach that suits their needs. These include creating an account, signing in with an existing account, continuing with Google and exploring as a guest.

Register a new account

If you are using Knovilla for the first time and would like a registered account, select the registration option. Complete the requested information, including your username, email address and password, along with any other fields displayed on the registration form.

Choose a password that is difficult for other people to guess. Before submitting the form, review your details to avoid mistakes that could make signing in difficult later.

Log in to an existing account

Already registered? Choose the login option and enter your account credentials. This is the appropriate route when returning to Knovilla with an account you previously created.

Continue with Google

You can also choose the Google authentication option. Follow the instructions presented during the sign-in process and select the Google account you want to use.

Make sure you are comfortable using the selected account before completing authentication.

Continue as a guest

If you would prefer to explore before registering, select Continue as Guest. Guest access offers an alternative way to enter Knovilla and become familiar with the application.

Keep in mind that guest access and registered account access may differ. If you intend to use Knovilla regularly, consider creating an account rather than assuming that information from a guest session will always remain available.

3. Explore the Home screen

After entering Knovilla, spend a little time exploring the Home screen before immediately starting an activity. Familiarising yourself with the available navigation makes it easier to understand where different features are located.

Look through the available sections and identify the activities that interest you. Knovilla combines educational content with interactive experiences, so different areas of the application may serve different purposes.

Some activities focus on answering academic questions, while others introduce a more game-oriented approach to participation. Read the labels and instructions presented on each screen rather than assuming that every activity follows the same rules.

If you are new to the application, beginning with a familiar subject can help you understand how its questions and controls work.

4. Choose a learning activity

Knovilla includes educational activities involving subjects such as Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics. Depending on the selected activity, you may encounter different educational levels, question formats or setup options.

Before starting, consider what you want to achieve. Are you revising a subject, practising how to answer questions, exploring an unfamiliar topic or participating in a quiz with other people?

Your goal should guide your selection. For example, someone revising Biology may prefer a subject-based question activity, while a group looking for a shared quiz experience may be interested in a multiplayer format.

Where an activity provides configuration options, review them carefully. These may include subject selection, educational level, question format, number of rounds or other settings relevant to that particular mode.

Do not select an educational level simply because it appears more advanced. Choose the level that corresponds to the material you actually want to practise.

5. Understand the question formats

Different assessment formats test knowledge in different ways. Understanding the format of an activity helps you prepare for the type of response expected.

Multiple-choice questions generally require you to select an answer from the available options. True-or-false questions ask you to determine whether a statement is correct. Theory-based questions may require a more detailed explanation, while practical-oriented activities focus on applying knowledge to relevant situations.

The formats available depend on the activity you choose. Always read the instructions shown before beginning a session.

During a quiz, pay attention to the question wording and any displayed timer. Avoid rushing through unfamiliar questions without first understanding what is being asked.

6. Complete your first activity

Once you have selected an activity and reviewed its setup, follow the on-screen instructions to begin.

For your first session, focus on learning how the interface works rather than trying to achieve a perfect result. Observe how questions are presented, how answers are submitted and how the activity progresses from one stage to another.

If an activity includes rounds, turns or a countdown, pay attention to those elements. They may influence how much time you have to respond or when another participant takes over.

Once the activity ends, review any results or feedback provided. Consider which questions you found easy and which areas may require further revision.

7. Follow your learning progress

Learning becomes more meaningful when you reflect on your performance over time. After completing supported activities, visit your profile to explore any available progress information associated with your account.

Progress information can help you think about your strengths and identify areas that deserve additional attention. However, remember that a single quiz result does not provide a complete measure of your understanding of a subject.

A useful learning routine is to practise, review your mistakes, revisit relevant study materials and attempt additional questions. Repeated practice can help reinforce knowledge and improve familiarity with different assessment formats.

8. Make the most of Knovilla

There is no need to explore every feature during your first visit. Start with one activity, become comfortable with its controls and gradually discover other parts of the platform.

If you are using Knovilla for academic revision, consider setting a clear objective for each session. You might decide to practise a particular subject, work through a question format or spend a short period reviewing material you previously found challenging.

Remember that educational applications are most useful when combined with active learning. Read your textbooks, consult your teachers where necessary and use quiz activities to reinforce what you have studied.

Knovilla is designed to make educational practice more interactive and accessible, but consistent participation and thoughtful revision remain important parts of the learning process.

Keep exploring

You now have a starting point for navigating Knovilla. Open the application, choose an entry option and try an activity that matches your learning goal.
