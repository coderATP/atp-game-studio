Welcome to the Knovilla Learning Guides

Knovilla is an educational application developed by ATP Game Studio. It brings together subject-based quizzes, interactive learning activities, multiplayer experiences and assessment tools in a browser-accessible environment.

Whether you are visiting Knovilla for the first time, preparing to participate in a multiplayer quiz or exploring its assessment features, these guides provide practical explanations to help you get started.

The articles below cover different aspects of the application, from account access and basic navigation to theory assessments, grading and reviewing previous questions. Each guide focuses on a particular activity, making it easier to find instructions relevant to what you want to accomplish.

You can read the guides in order or select a topic directly. New users may find it helpful to begin with the introductory guide before exploring more specialised features.

Which Knovilla Guide Should You Read First?

The best starting point depends on your familiarity with the application and what you want to achieve. If you have never used Knovilla, begin with Getting Started with Knovilla . It introduces the available entry options and explains how to approach your first activity.

If you are interested in playing with friends or classmates, the multiplayer guides explain three different approaches: sharing one device through local pass-and-play, participating over a local Wi-Fi connection and joining an online multiplayer experience.

Learners preparing for written assessments can explore the theory assessment guide, while teachers and examiners may find the theory grading walkthrough more relevant to their responsibilities.

For returning users, the profile and progress guide provides information about managing account-related features. The multiplayer question review guide also offers a useful starting point for reflecting on previous activities and learning from mistakes.

Make Your Learning Sessions More Productive

Educational applications can be useful tools for reinforcing knowledge, but getting the most from them requires more than simply answering questions. Before beginning a session, think about the subject or skill you want to practise.

Start with material that corresponds to your current learning needs. Read questions carefully, pay attention to the instructions for each activity and review your performance whenever relevant feedback is available.

If you encounter unfamiliar concepts, consider returning to your textbooks, classroom notes or other reliable educational materials. Combining independent study with interactive practice can help make your learning sessions more purposeful.

Knovilla's different activities offer multiple ways to engage with academic content. You do not have to explore every feature immediately. Begin with the areas that interest you and gradually discover additional activities as you become more comfortable with the platform.
