How to Host or Join a Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer Game on Knovilla

In Game Mode, open local multiplayer and select Local Wi-Fi.

Get started

In Game Mode, open local multiplayer and select Local Wi-Fi.

Configure or prepare

To host, configure players, difficulty, rounds, subjects, question format, and turn duration, then create a room.

Continue through the activity

Share the displayed joining details. Other participants select Join and enter the requested room information.

Complete the next step

Check the lobby for connected players and the selected configuration. Wait for everyone before the host starts the game.

Review and finish

Remain connected during questions, turns, and score updates. At the end, examine rankings and review attempted questions.

Keep exploring

Try these steps in Knovilla or continue to the next guide.
