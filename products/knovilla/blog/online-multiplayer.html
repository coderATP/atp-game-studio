How to Play Online Multiplayer Quizzes on Knovilla

Open Game Mode and choose Online Multiplayer. Select Host to create a session or Join to enter an existing room.

Get started

Open Game Mode and choose Online Multiplayer. Select Host to create a session or Join to enter an existing room.

Configure or prepare

The host configures players, difficulty, rounds, subjects, question format, and turn duration, then shares the joining details.

Continue through the activity

Wait in the lobby until the intended participants have connected. The host starts the game when ready.

Complete the next step

Play through the questions and countdowns, following the on-screen turns and score updates.

Review and finish

At the end, inspect rankings and select Review Questions to see attempts, timeouts, correct answers, awarded points, and available explanations.

Keep exploring

Try these steps in Knovilla or continue to the next guide.
