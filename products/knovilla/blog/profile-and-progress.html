How to Track Your Learning Progress and Manage Your Knovilla Profile

Get started

Sign in and open Profile to access your account details and available learning progress.

Configure or prepare

Explore recorded progress, achievements, streaks, and daily challenges where available for your activities.

Continue through the activity

Choose an avatar or upload a profile image. Use the cropping interface to adjust an uploaded picture before saving.

Complete the next step

Open profile editing to manage supported account information. Available email and password options can vary by authentication method.

Review and finish

Use account actions to sign out or request account deletion, which includes confirmation. Revisit your progress to guide future practice.
