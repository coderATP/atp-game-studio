How to Track Your Learning Progress and Manage Your Knovilla Profile

Knovilla brings your learning activity, game performance, daily challenges and achievements together in one place. Its Profile screen gives you a quick overview of your journey, while the Progress Dashboard provides a more detailed breakdown of your quiz results and multiplayer matches.

This guide explains how to interpret those records, use them to identify areas for improvement, and manage the information associated with your account.

1. Start with Your Profile Overview

Open your Profile screen to see three headline statistics: your current day streak, completed quizzes and completed games. These figures offer a quick snapshot of your recent learning activity without requiring you to inspect every individual result.

  • Day Streak: Shows your current study streak. The displayed value can return to zero when the recorded study date is more than one calendar day behind or is in the future.
  • Quizzes: Summarises completed quizzes across the supported assessment formats.
  • Games: Summarises completed matches across Knovilla's supported Game Mode types.

The profile overview is intentionally brief. For a more detailed picture of your performance, select View Progress to open the Progress Dashboard.

2. Understand the Progress Dashboard

The dashboard has two main tabs: Test Mode and Game Mode. Each focuses on a different kind of activity, so choose the tab that matches what you want to review.

Test Mode: Your assessment record

The Overall Summary compares your recorded results across MCQ, True/False, Theory and Practical formats. Depending on the format, you can inspect completed quizzes, questions answered, correct answers, accuracy, study time, topics covered and streak statistics.

Some metrics do not apply equally to every format. For example, the dashboard does not display a numerical correct-answer count for Theory or Practical in the summary tables. Use the figures provided for each format rather than assuming every assessment is scored in the same way.

You can also open Subject Mastery (General) to compare your recorded activity by subject, or Subject Mastery (Grade / Level) to examine a subject within a particular grade or level. These tables include measures such as quizzes taken, questions answered, correct answers where applicable, accuracy and time spent.

This distinction helps you look beyond an overall score. You may, for example, have strong results in one subject but need more practice in another, or perform differently in the same subject at different levels.

Game Mode: Your multiplayer performance

Switch to Game Mode to review completed matches in Local Pass & Play, Local Wi-Fi and Online Multiplayer. Open a game-mode card to explore its recorded statistics.

The overview includes completed games, total questions, correct and wrong answers, timed-out questions, accuracy and total players. Additional sections break results down by MCQ and True/False formats, and by Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme difficulty.

Finishing-position statistics are also available. Pass & Play reports positions across players in completed matches, while the other modes show your recorded finishing positions. These are useful for evaluating competitive performance alongside accuracy.

3. Review Your Recent Activity

The bottom of each dashboard tab displays a moving summary of recent activity. In Test Mode, entries can include the date, time, subject, assessment format, score, accuracy and time spent. Theory and Practical scores are presented against total marks, while other formats use the total number of questions.

In Game Mode, recent match entries can show the date, time, game mode, difficulty, question format, correct answers, accuracy and finishing position when available. The display includes up to 20 recent game records.

Use these summaries to spot patterns. If your accuracy has fallen, revisit the relevant material before moving to more difficult questions. If you frequently time out during games, practise answering within the available time. When results improve, compare them with your earlier attempts to see whether your practice is helping.

4. Complete Daily Challenges

Your Profile screen includes a Today's Challenges section. Each challenge has a progress indicator showing how much of its target you have completed. The section also displays the number of completed challenges against the total available.

A completed challenge is marked accordingly, and a message appears when all the day's challenges are done. Check this section regularly if you want a clear, manageable target for your learning session.

5. Work Towards Achievements

The Achievements section tracks your achievement collection and displays your overall completion percentage. When achievements have been unlocked, the profile can show recently earned badges, their descriptions and unlock dates.

If you have not unlocked an achievement yet, the section encourages you to build your activity through quizzes, study streaks and games. It can also highlight a next achievement, including its requirements and your current progress towards the target.

Treat achievements as milestones rather than substitutes for learning. The more useful question is not simply whether you have earned a badge, but whether your understanding and assessment performance are improving.

6. Update Your Profile and Account

Registered users can select Edit Profile to manage their account details. The available options include changing your username and full name, checking your email verification status, requesting an email verification message, and changing your password when the account supports password-based sign-in.

An email change can be requested by entering a new address and your current password. The new address must be valid and different from the existing one. You must then follow the confirmation process sent to the new address; the existing email remains active until the change is confirmed.

Accounts that use Google sign-in without a password have different restrictions. The current interface explains that password changes are unavailable for these accounts unless password-based authentication is supported, and changing their email address through this screen is not currently available.

Change your profile picture

Registered users can open the camera control on their Profile screen to choose from the available built-in avatars or upload an image. Uploaded profile images must be image files smaller than 5 MB and pass through an image-cropping step before upload. If a picture is already set, you can also choose to remove it.

Guest sessions have a more limited profile interface: profile-picture controls and profile editing are not available in the same way as they are for registered users.

7. Protect Your Account

The Edit Profile screen includes a Data & Account section with an option to begin the account-deletion process. Deleting an account is a significant action, so make sure you understand the consequences before proceeding.

When you are ready to leave your account, use the Logout option on your Profile screen and confirm the action. Guest users see an option to leave their guest session instead. On shared devices, always leave your session when you have finished.

Make Your Progress Useful

Knovilla's dashboard is most valuable when you use its statistics to decide what to do next. Review assessment results to find subjects that need more attention, use grade-level tables to identify differences in performance, and check Game Mode statistics to understand accuracy, timeouts and finishing positions.

Combine those observations with daily challenges and achievement targets to give your practice a clear direction. Progress is not just the number of quizzes completed or games won; it is also what you learn from each attempt.
