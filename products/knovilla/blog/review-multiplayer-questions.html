How to Review Multiplayer Questions and Learn from Your Mistakes on Knovilla

Discover how to review questions after a multiplayer quiz, understand your answers and use the results to prepare for future learning activities.

Completing a multiplayer quiz is only one part of the learning experience. Although the final scoreboard shows how participants performed, reviewing individual questions can provide a clearer understanding of what happened during the session.

Knovilla includes a question review feature for supported multiplayer activities. It allows participants to revisit attempted questions, examine submitted responses, identify correct answers and understand how points were awarded.

This guide explains how to access that information and use it constructively rather than focusing exclusively on final rankings.

1. Complete a supported multiplayer game

Begin by opening Knovilla and participating in a supported multiplayer quiz. Follow the game's instructions, answer questions during your turns and continue until the configured rounds finish.

At the end of the session, Knovilla presents the final results, including player rankings, scores and a summary of the completed game.

Take a moment to examine the scoreboard. However, remember that a player's position does not necessarily explain which questions they found difficult or why certain points were lost.

2. Open Review Questions

From the results screen, select Review Questions. This opens the question review interface, where you can examine cards representing attempted questions and their associated participants.

Move through the available review cards and read the information displayed for each attempt. Instead of rushing through the questions, pay attention to the original question, the submitted response and the correct answer.

This process can be especially useful when several participants encountered unfamiliar topics during the same game.

3. Understand the answer status

Each reviewed attempt includes a status indicating its outcome. Knovilla uses three labels:

  • Correct: The submitted response matches the correct answer.
  • Incorrect: The submitted response does not match the correct answer.
  • Time's Up: The participant did not complete the response before the permitted countdown expired.

These distinctions matter because an incorrect response and a timeout are not necessarily the same learning problem.

An incorrect answer may indicate a misunderstanding of the topic, while a timeout could suggest difficulty reading, recalling information or making a decision within the available time.

4. Compare submitted and correct answers

For each question, compare the participant's selected response with the correct answer displayed by Knovilla.

If your response was incorrect, try to identify why you originally selected it. Did you misunderstand the question, overlook an important detail or confuse two related concepts?

Avoid memorising only the position of the correct option. Understanding the underlying concept is more useful because similar questions may be presented differently in future activities.

You can also revisit questions answered correctly. Being able to explain why an answer is correct is a useful way to test your understanding.

5. Examine awarded points and explanations

The review interface includes the points awarded for individual attempts. Use this information to understand how different responses contributed to the final scores.

Where explanations are available, read them carefully. An explanation can provide additional context and help clarify the reasoning behind a correct answer.

If a topic remains unclear after reviewing the explanation, consult your textbooks, classroom notes or another reliable educational resource.

Consider discussing challenging questions with your teacher or fellow learners when additional clarification is needed.

6. Return to the results or start another session

Once you have finished reviewing the questions, select Back to Results to return to the multiplayer scoreboard.

If you would like to explore another activity, choose Back to Game Mode.

Before beginning another session, consider whether you should revise any of the topics identified during your review. A short period of focused study may be more beneficial than immediately repeating several quizzes without examining previous mistakes.

Turn Your Quiz Results into Better Learning Habits

Question review becomes more valuable when you use the information to guide future practice. After each multiplayer session, identify a few questions that challenged you and revisit the corresponding topics.

Pay attention to repeated mistakes. If you frequently struggle with similar questions, spend additional time studying the relevant concepts before attempting another quiz.

For timed activities, practise reading questions carefully while becoming more comfortable with the available response period.

Remember that multiplayer rankings represent performance during a particular session. They should encourage participation and improvement rather than discourage learners who are still developing their understanding.
