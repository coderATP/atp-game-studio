How to Review Multiplayer Questions and Learn from Your Mistakes on Knovilla

Complete a supported multiplayer game and inspect the final rankings, scores, and session summary.

Get started

Complete a supported multiplayer game and inspect the final rankings, scores, and session summary.

Configure or prepare

Select Review Questions to open cards for the attempted questions and their associated participants.

Continue through the activity

Compare selected and correct answers. Each attempt is marked Correct, Incorrect, or Time's Up, with points awarded.

Complete the next step

Read explanations where provided, focusing on why an answer is correct rather than memorising an option.

Review and finish

Choose Back to Results to return to the scoreboard, or Back to Game Mode for another session.

Keep exploring

Try these steps in Knovilla or continue to the next guide.
