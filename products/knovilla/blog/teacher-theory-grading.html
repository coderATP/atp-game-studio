How Teachers Can Review and Grade Theory Submissions on Knovilla

Sign in with an account that has the examiner role, then open the teacher dashboard.

Get started

Sign in with an account that has the examiner role, then open the teacher dashboard.

Configure or prepare

Browse active or archived submissions and choose the grading action for a submission.

Continue through the activity

Review the student's answers, including supported images, against the question and marking information.

Complete the next step

Enter marks for each part within its maximum score and add useful examiner comments.

Review and finish

Save the grading to the backend, then use the dashboard's result-sending action to send the graded result. Archive or restore records as needed.
