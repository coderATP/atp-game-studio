How to Take a Theory Assessment on Knovilla

Open the relevant assessment section, select a theory activity, and complete its setup.

Get started

Open the relevant assessment section, select a theory activity, and complete its setup.

Configure or prepare

Read every question and its parts carefully. Enter responses using the available controls; supported questions may allow written, structured, or image-upload answers.

Continue through the activity

Watch the remaining time. Use navigation and question-jump controls to move between questions and revisit answers.

Complete the next step

Where local saved data is available, an interrupted assessment can restore supported progress and uploaded-answer references. Avoid closing an active attempt unnecessarily.

Review and finish

Submit your work to open the theory result interface. Use the available review to inspect your submission and grading feedback when provided.

Keep exploring

Try these steps in Knovilla or continue to the next guide.
