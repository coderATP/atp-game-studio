How to Take a Theory Assessment on Knovilla

Learn how to navigate theory questions, enter different types of answers, use helpful assessment tools and submit your work through Knovilla.

Theory assessments give you an opportunity to demonstrate what you understand in your own words. Depending on the questions provided, you may need to explain a scientific concept, complete a table, write a calculation or upload an image of your work.

Knovilla's theory interface brings these activities together with question navigation, a countdown timer, a built-in calculator and progress-saving functionality. Understanding these tools before you begin can make an assessment easier to manage.

1. Start your theory assessment

Open Knovilla and navigate to the relevant assessment activity. Complete the setup and start the theory quiz.

Once the assessment opens, the top bar identifies the theory activity and your selected grade where that information is available. The screen also displays the remaining time, a submission button and controls for moving between questions.

Take a moment to familiarise yourself with these controls before concentrating on the questions. Knowing where to find them will help you navigate the assessment without unnecessary interruptions.

2. Read the question and inspect its materials

Each question may contain text, an illustration or additional information arranged around its individual parts. Read the entire question before beginning your response.

If an illustration is difficult to see, you can tap or click a supported question image to open a larger view. Tap or click the enlarged image to close it.

The interface also displays the marks available for individual question parts. Use this information to understand how the question is organised and what each part requires.

3. Enter answers using the appropriate controls

Knovilla supports different response formats within theory assessments. The controls shown depend on the type of answer required.

Written answers

For a written response, enter your explanation in the provided text area. Keep your answer relevant to the question and organise your explanation so that the examiner can follow your reasoning.

Table-based answers

Some questions allow you to construct a table. Use the row and column controls to choose its dimensions, then select Create Table. Enter the appropriate headings and information into the generated cells.

Check that your entries appear under the correct headings before moving on.

Image and combined answers

Where image uploads are supported, select an image from your device. For questions that accept both written and image responses, provide the required text and attach your image. Ensure that uploaded work is clear and relevant.

4. Use the calculator when necessary

A calculator button is available in the assessment's top bar. Select it to open the built-in calculator when you need help with a calculation.

This can be useful for numerical questions in subjects such as Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry.

A calculator does not replace the need to understand the question. Read the instructions carefully and provide any explanation or working required by the assessment.

5. Navigate between questions

Use Previous and Next to move between questions. The progress indicator shows your current position, such as question 3 of 10.

If you want to move directly to a particular question, select Jump To. A numbered grid lets you choose the question you want to open.

The grid visually distinguishes the current question and indicates which questions appear to have answers and which remain unanswered. Use it to identify questions you may want to revisit.

The countdown pauses while the question-jump window is open. Close the window or select a question to return to the assessment.

6. Keep track of your time and saved answers

The timer displays the remaining assessment time in hours, minutes and seconds. Check it periodically so you can distribute your attention across the available questions.

Knovilla saves assessment progress locally as you work, including supported written answers, table entries and references to uploaded files. This information can help restore a supported interrupted attempt.

However, local saving is not a guarantee that every interruption can be recovered. Avoid closing the assessment unnecessarily, and check any restored answers carefully before continuing.

When the countdown reaches zero, the application initiates submission automatically. Do not rely on the timer expiring as your preferred way to finish.

7. Review and submit your work

Before finishing, revisit unanswered or uncertain questions using the navigation controls. Check your written responses, table entries and uploaded images.

When you are ready, select SUBMIT. Knovilla asks you to confirm that you want to submit your assessment. Read the prompt carefully before confirming.

After confirmation, the application prepares your answers and uploads the submission. Wait for this process to finish rather than attempting to submit repeatedly.

Successful submission takes you to the theory result interface, where the available submission information and subsequent review options can be accessed.

What Happens After Submission?

Theory responses may require an examiner to review the submitted work and assign marks. Therefore, submitting an assessment does not necessarily mean that a final grade is immediately available.

When grading feedback becomes available, read the marks and comments carefully. Use them to identify concepts you understand well and areas that need further study.

A useful assessment experience involves more than finishing before the timer expires. Read carefully, use the available tools, check your answers and learn from the feedback provided.
