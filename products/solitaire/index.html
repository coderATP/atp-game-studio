ABOUT THE GAME

A familiar game with room for strategy.

Klondike Solitaire is a single-player card game built around a simple objective: move every card into the four foundation piles. The rules are easy to understand, but choosing the right move can require careful planning.

Cards hidden beneath the tableau can only be revealed by moving the cards above them. At the same time, you need to manage the draw and discard piles, create useful sequences and decide when a card should move to a foundation.

The basic rule

HOW TO PLAY

Build your way to all four Kings.

01

Reveal the tableau

Start by examining the seven tableau piles. Only the available face-up cards can normally be moved. Look for opportunities to uncover hidden cards.

02

Build sequences

Arrange tableau cards downward by rank while alternating colours. Valid face-up sequences can be moved together when they can legally fit.

03

Use the draw pile

Draw cards when you need new possibilities. The top card of the discard pile becomes available for play.

04

Build the foundations

Move Aces to their corresponding foundations and continue upward using cards of the same suit.

05

Manage empty spaces

An empty tableau pile can only receive a King or a valid sequence beginning with a King. Creating an empty space can greatly increase flexibility.

06

Complete every foundation

Keep organizing, revealing and transferring cards until all four foundations have been completed from Ace through King.

DRAW & DISCARD

Every draw can change the board.

The draw pile gives you access to cards that have not yet entered the tableau. Drawing too quickly can hide useful possibilities, so consider your available moves before taking the next card.

01 Tap the draw pile to move its top card into the discard pile.
02 The top discard card can be moved when a legal move is available.
03 When the draw pile is empty, recycle the discard pile to draw again.

THE REAL PUZZLE

Look beneath the visible cards.

One of the most important goals in Klondike Solitaire is revealing the face-down cards hidden in the tableau.

01

Find opportunities

Look for moves that remove a face-up card from the top of a tableau pile.

02

Reveal what is hidden

Moving the available cards away can expose a new face-down card and create another possible move.

03

Create useful space

An empty tableau pile can accept a King, potentially giving you more room to reorganize the board.

STRATEGY

Play the move, not just the card.

Klondike Solitaire rewards planning. A legal move is not necessarily the best move.

01

Reveal hidden cards

When several legal moves are available, consider which one exposes a face-down card.

02

Protect useful cards

Do not automatically move every available card to a foundation if keeping it in the tableau could create a useful sequence.

03

Think before drawing

Consider the available tableau and foundation moves before taking another card from the draw pile.

04

Use empty spaces wisely

Creating an empty tableau pile can provide room for a King and open new ways to reorganize cards.

05

Plan several moves ahead

The first legal move you see may not be the move that produces the strongest position.

06

Use Undo intelligently

Experiment with different sequences and reverse a move when it produces a worse position.
